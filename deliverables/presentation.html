Motivation

Form processing is the second-largest IDP workload in DACH Mittelstand after invoices. Insurance, HR, legal, and clinical-trial workflows all depend on extracting question-answer pairs from non-uniform forms (Schadenmeldung, Lohnsteuerbescheinigung, Krankenkasse-Antrag, CRF, patient intake). Manual entry costs 2-6 EUR per page; an automated pipeline targets greater than 90% straight-through processing on the four FUNSD-style entity classes.

Why text-only fails on forms. The token "Date" can be a header, a question, or an answer caption depending on its 2D position. A reading-order linearisation throws away exactly the signal needed to disambiguate it. This is the gap that LayoutLM-family models close.

Dataset

FUNSD (Form Understanding in Noisy Scanned Documents, Jaume 2019) is the canonical benchmark for layout-aware document tagging. The dataset is small (~16 MB) but expressive: each entity has a bounding box, the text content, a four-way label, word-level boxes, and explicit linking edges to other entities.

BIO label scheme. Four entity classes are mapped to seven effective tags: O, B-question, I-question, B-answer, I-answer, B-header, I-header. Words inside other entities are mapped to O.

Methods

Baseline - BERT-base, layout ignored

  • HuggingFace bert-base-uncased with a token-classification head.
  • Words flattened in annotation order (proxy for reading order).
  • WordPiece tokenisation, max-length 512, AdamW, lr 5e-5, 10 epochs, batch 8.
  • Saves model + metrics to deliverables/.

Advanced - LayoutLMv3 with bbox + image patches

  • HuggingFace microsoft/layoutlmv3-base with apply_ocr=False (gold tokens supplied).
  • Bounding boxes normalised to a 0-1000 grid; image split into 16x16 patches.
  • Multi-task head: token classification + binary linking-edge classifier (q-a entity pairs).
  • AdamW, lr 3e-5, 5 epochs, batch 4, FP16 on CUDA.
  • LiLT (Wang 2022) documented as a multilingual swap-in for the layout backbone.

Metrics

  • Per-class precision, recall, F1 over the 7 BIO tags.
  • Macro-F1 (unweighted mean) as headline metric.
  • Linking F1 on undirected (q, a) pair matching, plus PR curve sweeping the threshold.

Discussion

What layout buys us

Bounding-box geometry plus image patches give a sizeable boost over text-only encoders on FUNSD-like tasks, with the largest gains on classes where surface text is ambiguous (header, short answer). Reported LayoutLMv3 F1 on FUNSD is approximately 0.92, vs approximately 0.80 for BERT-base.

FUNSD is small

149 training forms is two orders of magnitude smaller than RVL-CDIP. Per-seed variance is 1-2 absolute F1 points; multi-seed averaging is recommended for production claims.

OCR noise

FUNSD provides hand-annotated tokens, so OCR is zero-noise. Real pipelines will lose 5-15 F1 points to Tesseract errors. Donut and Pix2Struct sidestep this with OCR-free image-to-text architectures, at 5-10x the inference cost.

Multilingual transfer

For German Behörden-Formulare and French CRF, the recommended path is LiLT paired with XLM-R, or LayoutXLM out of the box. Fine-tuning on 50-100 native gold forms is sufficient for production deployment.

Engineering pitfalls

  • apply_ocr=False on the LayoutLMv3 processor is essential when feeding pre-OCR'd tokens, otherwise Tesseract silently overrides them.
  • Image patches dominate GPU memory; per-batch padding to longest form (not 512) saves 30-50% compute.
  • FP16 inference is free F1 on Ampere or Hopper GPUs.

Conclusion
